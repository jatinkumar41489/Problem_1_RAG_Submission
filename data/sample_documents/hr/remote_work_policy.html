Meridian Cloud Remote Work Policy

Meridian Cloud operates a hybrid working model. This policy explains who may work remotely, which rules apply, and what equipment support is available.

Eligibility

Employees become eligible for hybrid working after completing the 90-day probation period. During probation, employees are expected to work from their assigned office. Roles that require physical access to data-center hardware are not eligible for remote work and are marked as on-site in the job description.

Hybrid schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The remaining days are spent in the office, and teams agree on common anchor days. Everyone must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Meetings with external customers should be scheduled inside core hours whenever possible.

Home office support

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $600 for a desk, chair or monitor. In addition, Meridian Cloud reimburses internet costs of up to $40 per month on submission of a bill. The stipend can be claimed once every 3 years.

Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 days per calendar year. Every request requires approval from both People Operations and the Security team, because tax, immigration and data-protection rules differ by country. Requests must be filed at least 4 weeks before the planned trip. Working from a country on the restricted list is never permitted.

Equipment and security

Company laptops must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems from home. Employees must not use personal computers to access production systems. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the Security team within 24 hours.
