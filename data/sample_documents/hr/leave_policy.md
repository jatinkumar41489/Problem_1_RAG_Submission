# Meridian Cloud Leave Policy

This policy describes the time-off entitlements of full-time employees at Meridian Cloud. Part-time employees receive the same entitlements pro rata. The policy is owned by the People Operations team and reviewed every January.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, so an employee who joins mid-year receives a proportional amount. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March. Any leave request longer than 10 consecutive working days needs written approval from a department head and must be submitted at least 6 weeks in advance. Requests of 10 days or fewer only need approval from the direct manager, who should respond within 3 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave each year. Sick days do not carry over. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Employees should notify their manager before the start of their working day, and no later than 10:00 local time, if they are unable to work because of illness.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption. After returning, an employee may work a gradual schedule of 80% hours at full pay for the first 4 weeks. Parental leave does not reduce annual leave entitlement, and annual leave continues to accrue while an employee is on parental leave.

## Public holidays and winter shutdown

Meridian Cloud observes 11 public holidays per year, published each November for the following year. The company also closes for a winter shutdown between 25 December and 1 January. The shutdown days are paid and do not count against annual leave. Employees who are on call during the shutdown are compensated under the on-call handbook.

## Sabbatical and unpaid leave

Employees with 5 years of continuous service may apply for a paid sabbatical of 4 weeks. Sabbaticals must be agreed with the department head at least 3 months in advance and cannot be combined with annual leave to exceed 6 consecutive weeks. Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted with approval from a vice president. Health insurance continues during approved unpaid leave, but leave accrual is paused.
