On-Call Handbook

This handbook explains how engineering teams at Meridian Cloud staff the on-call rotation for production services and what is expected from the person on call.

Rotation

Each service team runs a weekly rotation with a primary and a secondary engineer. Handover happens every Monday at 10:00 local time and the outgoing engineer writes a handover note covering open incidents and risky changes. No engineer may be primary on call for more than 1 week in every 4.

Acknowledgement targets

When an alert pages the primary engineer, it must be acknowledged within 5 minutes for a SEV1 incident and within 15 minutes for a SEV2 incident. If the primary does not acknowledge a SEV1 page within 5 minutes, it escalates automatically to the secondary engineer, and after a further 10 minutes to the engineering manager. SEV3 alerts are handled during business hours and never page at night.

During an incident

The first responder opens an incident channel, posts a status update every 30 minutes, and assigns an incident lead if the incident lasts more than an hour. Customer-facing status page updates are posted by the support team on request of the incident lead. Engineers should prefer mitigation, such as a rollback, over a long investigation while customers are affected.

Compensation and rest

Engineers receive an on-call stipend of $500 per week of primary rotation. A page that requires work between 00:00 and 06:00 earns a compensatory day off, to be taken within 14 days. Engineers on call during the winter shutdown receive double the weekly stipend.

Reducing alert noise

Each team reviews its alerts monthly. Any alert that paged more than 3 times in a month without requiring action must be tuned or deleted. Runbook links are mandatory for every paging alert.
